# Hoth

Three years after the destruction of the Death Star, the Galactic Empire is hunting the Rebel Alliance across the galaxy. Darth Vader has sent probe droids out in every direction to find the hidden Rebel base.

One probe droid lands on Hoth, a frozen planet where the Rebels have built Echo Base under the ice.

Luke Skywalker is out on patrol on a tauntaun when he sees what looks like a meteor come down. Before he can reach it, a wampa knocks him from his mount and drags him back to its ice cave.

Back at Echo Base, Han Solo tells the Rebel leaders he is leaving to pay off the bounty Jabba the Hutt has on his head. Princess Leia is cold about it, and the two argue. Han says she wants him to stay, which she denies.

In the wampa's cave, Luke hangs upside down from the ceiling. He uses the Force to pull his lightsaber out of the snow, cuts himself free, and wounds the wampa before stumbling out into a blizzard.

When Luke does not come back, Han rides out alone on a tauntaun to find him, even though the base doors will be shut for the night.

Luke collapses in the snow. The ghost of Obi-Wan Kenobi appears to him and tells him to go to the Dagobah system, where he will learn from Yoda, the Jedi Master who taught Obi-Wan.

Han finds Luke as the tauntaun dies of cold. He cuts it open and puts Luke inside to keep him warm, then sets up a shelter. Rebel snowspeeders find them both alive the next morning.

Han and Chewbacca spot the probe droid near the base. When Han shoots at it, the droid destroys itself, but it has already signalled the Empire. The Rebels begin to evacuate Hoth.

On his flagship, Vader recognises the image of the base. He orders the fleet to Hoth with Admiral Ozzel in command and Captain Piett at his side.

Ozzel brings the fleet out of hyperspace too close to the planet, which warns the Rebels in time to raise their energy shield. Vader chokes Ozzel to death over the viewscreen and promotes Piett to Admiral Piett in his place.

With the shield blocking any attack from space, General Veers leads a ground assault of AT-AT walkers against the shield generator.

Luke leads the snowspeeders against the walkers. His gunner Dak is killed, but the pilots learn to trip the walkers with tow cables. Luke is shot down and brings a walker down on his own with a grenade.

Veers destroys the shield generator, and snowtroopers storm Echo Base. Vader walks in to find the Rebels gone. Transports escape under cover from the base's ion cannon.

Han, Leia, Chewbacca and C-3PO escape on the Millennium Falcon, but its hyperdrive is broken. Luke and R2-D2 leave in his X-wing, and instead of joining the fleet, Luke sets a course for Dagobah.

# The escape

Star Destroyers and TIE fighters chase the Falcon into an asteroid field. Han lands in a cave on a large asteroid to hide and work on the hyperdrive.

While they make repairs, Han and Leia argue again, and he kisses her before C-3PO interrupts. Mynocks attach themselves to the ship. When Han fires into the cave, the ground moves, and they realise the cave is the mouth of a giant space slug. They fly out just before it closes.

Luke crash-lands his X-wing in a swamp on Dagobah, a misty planet full of jungle. A small green creature turns up at his camp, goes through his supplies, and fights R2-D2 over a lamp.

The creature says it knows Yoda and will take Luke to him, and leads him to its little house for supper.

The Emperor contacts Vader by hologram. He says the son of Skywalker is a new enemy who must not become a Jedi. Vader proposes turning Luke to the dark side instead.

Vader hires bounty hunters, Boba Fett among them, to find the Falcon, and warns Boba Fett he wants the passengers alive.

The Falcon flies out of the asteroid field with the hyperdrive still broken, then turns and clamps onto the back of Captain Needa's Star Destroyer, where its sensors cannot see it. Needa reports to Vader that the ship has vanished and apologises. Vader kills him.

When the Imperial fleet dumps its garbage before jumping to hyperspace, the Falcon drifts away with it. Boba Fett has guessed the trick and follows.

# Dagobah

In his house, the creature grows impatient with Luke, and Obi-Wan's voice answers him. The creature is Yoda.

Yoda doubts Luke can be trained. He says Luke is reckless, too old, and always looking to the future instead of where he is. Obi-Wan persuades him to try.

Yoda trains Luke hard in the swamp, riding on his back as he runs, climbs and flips. He teaches that the dark side of the Force is quicker and easier but not stronger.

Luke senses something cold in the cave under the dead tree. Yoda tells him to go in, and that he will not need his weapons. Luke takes them anyway.

Inside, Luke meets Darth Vader and cuts off his head. The helmet breaks open, and the face inside is Luke's own.

Luke's X-wing sinks deeper into the swamp. Luke tries to lift it with the Force and gives up, saying it is too big. Yoda lifts it out of the water himself, and Luke cannot believe it. Yoda says that is why he fails.

# Cloud City

Han sets course for Bespin, where his old friend Lando Calrissian runs Cloud City, a mining colony floating in the clouds. Lando once lost the Falcon to Han in a card game.

Lando welcomes them, and his aide Lobot shows them around. C-3PO wanders off, walks into a room full of stormtroopers, and is shot to pieces. Chewbacca finds his parts in a scrap room and starts putting him back together.

While training, Luke has a vision of Han and Leia in pain in a city in the clouds. Yoda and Obi-Wan tell him to stay and finish his training, and warn that he is not ready to face Vader. Luke leaves anyway, promising to come back.

As Luke flies off, Obi-Wan says that boy was their last hope. Yoda answers that there is another.

Lando takes Han, Leia and Chewbacca to dinner, and Darth Vader is waiting at the table. Han fires at him, and Vader catches the shots in his hand.

Lando says he had no choice. He made a deal with Vader to keep the Empire out of Cloud City. Vader has Han tortured to draw Luke to Bespin. Boba Fett is there to claim Han for Jabba's bounty.

Vader plans to freeze Luke in carbonite to take him to the Emperor, and orders it tested on Han first. In the freezing chamber, Leia tells Han she loves him, and he answers that he knows. Han is frozen solid and handed to Boba Fett.

Lando realises Vader keeps changing the deal. Lobot and Lando's guards free Leia, Chewbacca and C-3PO. Chewbacca nearly strangles Lando before letting him explain.

They run to stop Boba Fett, but his ship takes off with Han aboard. Lando orders the city evacuated, and the group escape on the Falcon.

Luke arrives and finds Vader in the carbon-freezing chamber. They fight, and Luke jumps clear of the freezing pit. Vader drives him out onto a gantry over a long shaft.

Vader cuts off Luke's right hand, and his lightsaber falls away down the shaft. Vader then tells Luke that he is his father, and asks Luke to join him so they can rule the galaxy together.

Luke refuses and lets himself fall down the shaft. He is swept out under the city and clings to a weather vane, calling out through the Force to Ben, and then to Leia.

Leia hears him and makes Lando turn the Falcon back. They pull Luke aboard. As Star Destroyers close in, R2-D2 fixes the hyperdrive, and the Falcon jumps to lightspeed. Luke hears Vader calling to him and calls him Father.

With the Rebel fleet, Luke is fitted with a mechanical hand. Lando and Chewbacca set off in the Falcon to find Han, and Luke and Leia watch them go.
