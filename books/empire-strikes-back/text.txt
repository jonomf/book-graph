# Hoth

Three years after the destruction of the Death Star, the Galactic Empire is hunting the Rebel Alliance across the galaxy. Darth Vader has sent probe droids out in every direction to find the hidden Rebel base. One probe droid lands on Hoth, a frozen planet where the Rebels have built Echo Base under the ice.

Luke Skywalker is out on patrol on a tauntaun when he sees what he takes for a meteorite come down. Before he can reach it, a wampa knocks him from his mount and drags him off to its ice cave.

Luke wakes hanging upside down from the ceiling of the wampa's cave while the creature eats his tauntaun. His lightsaber lies in the snow out of reach. He uses the Force to pull it into his hand, cuts himself free, slices off the wampa's arm and stumbles out into a rising blizzard.

Back at Echo Base, Han Solo tells General Rieekan he is leaving to pay off the bounty Jabba the Hutt has on his head. Princess Leia is cold about it, and the two argue. Han says she wants him to stay, which she denies. Then word comes that Luke has not come back. Han takes a tauntaun and rides out alone to find him, even though the base doors will be shut for the night.

Luke collapses in the snow. The ghost of Obi-Wan Kenobi appears to him and tells him to go to the Dagobah system, where he will learn from Yoda, the Jedi Master who taught Obi-Wan. Han finds Luke as his tauntaun dies of cold. He cuts it open with Luke's lightsaber and puts Luke inside to keep him warm, then sets up a shelter for the night.

At dawn, the snowspeeder pilot Zev Senesca finds them both alive. Back at the base, the medical droid 2-1B heals Luke in a bacta tank. Han teases Leia about her feelings for him, and to spite him she kisses Luke.

Han and Chewbacca go out to check a strange signal and find the probe droid. When Han shoots at it, the droid destroys itself, but it has already signalled the Empire. General Rieekan orders the Rebels to begin evacuating Hoth.

On the Executor, Vader's flagship, Captain Piett brings Vader the probe's pictures. Admiral Ozzel says they show nothing important, but Vader recognises the Rebel base and orders the fleet to Hoth.

Ozzel brings the fleet out of hyperspace too close to the planet, which warns the Rebels in time to raise their energy shield. Vader chokes Ozzel to death over the viewscreen and promotes Piett to Admiral Piett in his place. With the shield blocking any attack from space, he sends General Veers to lead a ground assault on the shield generator.

# The Battle of Hoth

Leia tells the pilots that each transport will leave with two fighters as escort. To clear a path through the blockade, the base fires its ion cannon at the nearest Star Destroyer, and the first transport gets away.

Luke leads Rogue Squadron's snowspeeders against the AT-AT walkers. His gunner Dak Ralter is killed. Wedge Antilles and his gunner Wes Janson wrap a tow cable around a walker's legs until it falls, and Rebel troops destroy it.

Luke is shot down and scrambles clear just before a walker crushes his snowspeeder. He grapples up under another walker, cuts open its belly with his lightsaber, and throws in a thermal detonator that brings it down.

Veers destroys the shield generator, and snowtroopers storm Echo Base. Leia orders the last of the Rebels to leave, and Han drags her to the Millennium Falcon when her own transport is cut off.

Vader walks into Echo Base just as the Millennium Falcon blasts its way out of the hangar.

Luke and R2-D2 take off in his X-wing. Instead of joining the fleet at the rendezvous, Luke sets a course for Dagobah. R2-D2 beeps his doubts the whole way.

# The escape

Star Destroyers and TIE fighters chase the Falcon, and its hyperdrive is broken. Han flies straight into an asteroid field while C-3PO lists the odds against them, then hides the ship in a cave on a large asteroid to make repairs.

Luke crash-lands his X-wing in a swamp on Dagobah, a misty planet full of jungle. Something in the water swallows R2-D2 and spits him back out. While Luke makes camp, a small green creature turns up, goes through his supplies, and fights R2-D2 over a lamp. The creature says it knows Yoda and will take Luke to him.

In the asteroid cave, Han and Leia argue again while they work on the ship. He kisses her, and she kisses him back, until C-3PO bursts in with news about the hyperdrive.

Vader orders his fleet to search the asteroid field, and an asteroid smashes into one of his Star Destroyers. The Emperor contacts Vader by hologram. He says the son of Skywalker is a new enemy who must not become a Jedi. Vader proposes turning Luke to the dark side instead, and vows that Luke will join them or die.

The creature leads Luke to its little house for supper. When Luke grows impatient, the creature talks to someone Luke cannot see, and Obi-Wan's voice answers. The creature is Yoda. Yoda doubts Luke can be trained. He says Luke is reckless, too old, and always looking to the future instead of where he is. Obi-Wan persuades him to try.

Mynocks are chewing on the Falcon's power cables. When Han fires into the cave, the ground moves, and they realise the cave is the mouth of a giant space slug. They fly out between its teeth just before it closes.

Vader hires bounty hunters, among them Boba Fett, Bossk, IG-88, Dengar, 4-LOM and Zuckuss, to find the Falcon, and warns Boba Fett he wants the passengers alive. Captain Needa's Star Destroyer chases the Falcon out of the asteroid field. The Falcon turns, flies straight at it, and clamps onto the back of the ship's command tower, where its sensors cannot see it.

Yoda trains Luke hard in the swamp, riding on his back as he runs, climbs and flips. He teaches that the dark side of the Force is quicker and easier but not stronger. Luke senses something cold in the cave under the dead tree. Yoda tells him to go in, and that he will not need his weapons. Luke takes them anyway. Inside, Luke meets Darth Vader and cuts off his head. The helmet breaks open, and the face inside is Luke's own.

Needa reports to Vader that the ship has vanished and apologises. Vader kills him. When the Imperial fleet dumps its garbage before jumping to hyperspace, the Falcon drifts away with it. Han decides to make for Bespin, where his old friend Lando Calrissian can fix the ship. Boba Fett has guessed the trick and follows.

Luke's X-wing sinks deeper into the swamp. Luke tries to lift it with the Force and gives up, saying it is too big. Yoda lifts it out of the water himself, and Luke cannot believe it. Yoda says that is why he fails.

# Cloud City

On Bespin, Lando Calrissian runs Cloud City, a mining colony floating in the clouds. Lando once lost the Falcon to Han in a card game. He welcomes them warmly, and his aide Lobot shows them around. Leia does not trust him.

C-3PO wanders off, walks into a room full of stormtroopers, and is shot to pieces. Ugnaughts carry his parts to a scrap room. Chewbacca finds them there and starts putting him back together.

While training, Luke has a vision of Han and Leia in pain in a city in the clouds. Yoda tells him it is the future, and that the future is always in motion. Luke wants to go to them at once.

Yoda and Obi-Wan tell Luke to stay and finish his training, and warn that he is not ready to face Vader. Luke leaves anyway, promising to come back. As he flies off, Obi-Wan says that boy was their last hope. Yoda answers that there is another.

Lando takes Han, Leia and Chewbacca to dinner, and Darth Vader is waiting at the table. Han fires at him, and Vader catches the shots in his hand. Lando says he had no choice. He made a deal with Vader to keep the Empire out of Cloud City. Boba Fett is there to claim Han for Jabba's bounty.

Vader has Han tortured, not to ask him anything but to draw Luke to Bespin. In their cell, Chewbacca puts C-3PO's head back on, facing backwards. Lando comes to tell them Vader has promised him Leia and Chewbacca, and that Vader really wants Luke. Han punches him.

Vader plans to freeze Luke in carbonite to take him to the Emperor, and orders it tested on Han first. In the carbon-freezing chamber, Leia tells Han she loves him, and he answers that he knows. Han is frozen solid and handed to Boba Fett, who will take him to Jabba the Hutt.

Vader then orders Leia and Chewbacca taken to his ship. Lando protests that this was not the deal, and realises Vader keeps changing it.

Luke lands on Cloud City and follows the sound of voices. Leia shouts a warning that it is a trap. Boba Fett fires at Luke, and Luke makes his way down to the carbon-freezing chamber, where Vader is waiting.

Lobot and Lando's guards free Leia, Chewbacca and C-3PO. Chewbacca nearly strangles Lando before letting him explain that there is still a chance to save Han. They run for the landing platform, but Boba Fett's ship takes off with Han aboard. Lando orders the city evacuated, R2-D2 rejoins them, and the group escape on the Falcon.

Luke duels Vader in the carbon-freezing chamber. When Vader tries to freeze him, Luke jumps clear of the freezing pit. Vader hurls machinery at him with the Force until a window bursts, and Luke is sucked out onto a gantry over a long shaft.

Vader cuts off Luke's right hand, and his lightsaber falls away down the shaft. Vader then tells Luke that he is his father. He asks Luke to join him, overthrow the Emperor and rule the galaxy together. Luke refuses and lets himself fall down the shaft.

Luke is sucked through a vent and blown out under the city, where he clings to a weather vane. He calls out through the Force to Ben, and then to Leia. Leia hears him and makes Lando turn the Falcon back, and Lando pulls Luke aboard.

The Falcon climbs out of Bespin's clouds, but the hyperdrive fails again as Vader's Star Destroyer closes in. Luke hears Vader calling to him and calls him Father. He asks why Ben never told him. R2-D2 fixes the hyperdrive, and the Falcon jumps to lightspeed.

With the Rebel fleet, 2-1B fits Luke with a mechanical hand. Lando and Chewbacca set off in the Falcon to find Han, and Luke and Leia watch them go with the droids.
